Constitutional Philosophy · Nov 4, 1948

Cultivating Constitutional Morality

"Constitutional morality is not a natural sentiment. It has to be cultivated. We must realize that our people have yet to learn it. Democracy in India is only a top-dressing on an Indian soil which is essentially undemocratic."

Final Constituent Assembly Address · Nov 25, 1949

Entering a Life of Contradictions

"On the 26th of January 1950, we are going to enter into a life of contradictions. In politics we will have equality and in social and economic life we will have inequality... We must remove this contradiction at the earliest possible moment."

Political democracy ('one person, one vote') cannot survive without social and economic democracy ('one person, one value').

BAWS Vol. 13 · Final Address to the Nation (November 25, 1949)

Fundamental Rights & Remedies

The Heart & Soul of the Constitution

"If I was asked to name any particular article in this Constitution as the most important — an article without which this Constitution would be a nullity — I could not refer to any other article except Article 32. It is the very soul of the Constitution and the very heart of it."
Constituent Assembly Debates · November 9, 1948

Masterwork of Social Theory · 1936

Annihilation of Caste

"Caste is not merely a division of labour. It is also a division of labourers... You cannot build anything on the foundations of caste. You cannot build up a nation, you cannot build up a morality."

Originally drafted for the Lahore Jat-Pat-Todak Mandal, this treatise remains the world's preeminent sociological dismantling of hereditary graded hierarchy.

BAWS Vol. 1 · Annihilation of Caste (1936)

Civil Rights Milestone · March 20, 1927

The Mahad Water Satyagraha

"It is not that you and I cannot live without drinking water from the Chavdar tank... We are going to the tank simply to establish our right that we are also human beings like others."

Celebrated annually as Social Empowerment Day (Samajik Adhikarita Divas) across India.

BAWS Vol. 17 · Mahad Historical Proceedings (Chavdar Tale)
🪙
Monetary Science · London School of Economics · 1923

The Problem of the Rupee & RBI

"A currency system should not merely provide a medium of exchange; it should provide a stable standard of value. If the standard varies, contract becomes gambling."

Dr. Ambedkar's doctoral evidence before the 1926 Royal Commission (Hilton Young Commission) provided the operational charter and design for the Reserve Bank of India (RBI Act 1934).

BAWS Vol. 6 · The Problem of the Rupee: Its Origin and Its Solution

Spiritual Emancipation · October 14, 1956

Deeksha Bhoomi, Nagpur

"My social philosophy may be said to be enshrined in three words: liberty, equality and fraternity. My philosophy has roots in religion and not in political science."

Over 500,000 followers embraced Navayana Buddhism at Nagpur, taking the 22 Vows of ethical liberation and human equality.

BAWS Vol. 11 · The Buddha and His Dhamma (1957)
👩
Gender Justice & Legal Emancipation · 1948–1951

Champion of Women's Rights

"I measure the progress of a community by the degree of progress which women have achieved."

Drafted the Hindu Code Bill granting daughters equal inheritance, abolishing polygamy, and legalizing divorce. Resigned from the Union Cabinet in September 1951 when conservative resistance stalled the bill.

BAWS Vol. 14 · Dr. Ambedkar and the Hindu Code Bill

All-India Depressed Classes Conference Address (July 1942)
