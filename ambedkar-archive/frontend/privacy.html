Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2024

The Ambedkar Digital Heritage Archive ("we", "our", "the Archive") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and (optionally) phone number for OTP authentication.
  • Research data: Bookmarks, reading progress, and notes you save are stored to provide a personalized research experience.
  • Usage data: We may collect non-personal data such as pages visited, search queries, and device type to improve the archive.
  • AI Assistant queries: Queries sent to the AI Research Assistant are processed to generate responses. We do not store chat history on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve archive services
  • To save your bookmarks and reading progress across sessions
  • To authenticate your account securely
  • To respond to support requests

3. Local Storage

We use localStorage in your browser to store your authentication token, language preference, and reading theme. No third-party tracking cookies are used. You may clear your browser's local storage at any time to remove this data.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google Sign-In: If you use Google authentication, your Google profile data (name, email) is processed according to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts: Fonts are served by Google Fonts. See Google Fonts Privacy.
  • Google Gemini AI: If AI responses are enabled, your queries are sent to Google's Gemini API. Queries are not used to train Google's models per Google's API Terms.

5. Data Security

Authentication tokens are signed with industry-standard JWT (JSON Web Tokens). Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. We do not store OTPs after use. We implement rate limiting to protect against abuse.

6. Data Retention

Account data is retained while your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.

7. Children's Privacy

This archive is designed for students, researchers, and the general public. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. Open Source Materials

All documents in this archive are from the public domain — the official Babasaheb Ambedkar Writings and Speeches (BAWS) published by the Ministry of External Affairs, Government of India, and the Dr. Ambedkar Foundation. These materials are freely available for research and educational purposes.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, please contact us through the archive's support channel.
