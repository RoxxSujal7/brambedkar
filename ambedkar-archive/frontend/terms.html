1. Purpose of the Archive

This archive is a public educational resource dedicated to preserving and providing access to the complete works of Dr. B. R. Ambedkar (1891–1956), including the official Babasaheb Ambedkar Writings and Speeches (BAWS) series published by the Ministry of External Affairs, Government of India.

2. Public Domain Content

The archival documents (BAWS volumes, speeches, letters, and historical records) are official government publications in the public domain, freely available for research, education, and personal use. Attribution to Dr. B. R. Ambedkar and the Dr. Ambedkar Foundation is appreciated but not legally required.

3. Permitted Uses

  • Browsing, reading, and downloading documents for personal study
  • Using content for academic research, teaching, and educational purposes
  • Citing passages with proper attribution in scholarly work
  • Sharing links to specific volumes or pages

4. Prohibited Uses

  • Misrepresenting quotes or misattributing words to Dr. Ambedkar
  • Using the AI Assistant for purposes unrelated to Ambedkar scholarship
  • Attempting to circumvent security measures, rate limits, or authentication
  • Automated scraping or bulk download without permission
  • Using the platform for commercial purposes without authorization
  • Submitting content designed to manipulate, mislead, or harm others

5. AI Research Assistant

The AI Research Assistant is designed to help you explore Dr. Ambedkar's works. Please note:

  • AI responses may contain errors. Always verify important claims against original source volumes.
  • The AI is grounded in the BAWS corpus but is not a substitute for primary source research.
  • Attempting to manipulate the AI to produce content unrelated to Ambedkar scholarship violates these terms.

6. OCR Manuscript Feature

The OCR tool is provided as a research aid. Results should be verified against original documents before use in scholarly work. The archive does not guarantee the accuracy of OCR outputs.

7. User Accounts

Research accounts are provided to enable features like bookmarks and reading progress. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your account credentials.

8. Disclaimer

9. Intellectual Heritage

Dr. Ambedkar's legacy belongs to all of humanity. This archive is maintained in the spirit of his commitment to education, equality, and the free exchange of knowledge.
