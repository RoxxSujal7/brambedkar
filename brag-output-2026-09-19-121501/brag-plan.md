# Brag Plan: Ambedkar Digital Heritage Archive (App Store Edition)

## What is this app?
The complete digital collection of Dr. B. R. Ambedkar's works, featuring 60 official BAWS volumes, 361 letters, historic constitutional debates, and an intelligent AI research assistant.

## The angle
Apple App Store product showcase. Clean, polished, benefit-driven product overview highlighting the user experience, search speed, and depth of archival content across web and mobile.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Clean product hero with app icon badge, crisp title, and clear category definition: "Ambedkar Heritage Archive • Complete Works & Research Platform".

## Key moments (the middle)
- Feature 1: Complete BAWS Library — 60 official volumes in English and Hindi with instant search.
- Feature 2: Historical Correspondence & Debates — 361 letters, speeches, and constituent assembly proceedings.
- Feature 3: AI Research Assistant & Manuscript OCR — semantic queries and instant citations.

## Outro / punchline
Clean App Store CTA: "Available Now • Free & Open Access at ambedkar-archive.org".

## User flow worth showing
Browse 60 Volumes -> Search Speeches & Letters -> Ask the AI Assistant.

## Tone
- Preset: app-store
- Creative direction: Sleek Apple-style feature tour and app showcase
- Interpretation: Crisp typography, title case, balanced cards, clean sliding transitions, and confident product presentation.
