# Brag Plan: Dr. B. R. Ambedkar Digital Heritage Archive (Institutional Heritage Edition)

## 1. What is this project?
The **Dr. B. R. Ambedkar Digital Heritage Archive** is a museum-grade digital collection and research platform preserving the complete intellectual, legal, and political corpus of Dr. Bhimrao Ramji Ambedkar. Engineered and curated by **Sujal Roxx**, it features 60 official BAWS volumes (20 English, 40 Hindi), 361 historic letters, the Constituent Assembly Debates (CAD), the 22 Dhamma vows, an interactive timeline, and an intelligent AI semantic research assistant with manuscript OCR.
