### 3. Show HN / Hacker News
**Show HN: Ambedkar Digital Heritage Archive – 60 Volumes, Debates & Semantic AI Search**

Hi HN,

I built the Dr. B. R. Ambedkar Digital Heritage Archive (https://ambedkar-archive.org) to make the complete 60-volume corpus of Dr. B. R. Ambedkar's writings, speeches, and constituent assembly debates instantly searchable and accessible.

**Tech stack & design decisions:**
- **Frontend:** Vanilla CSS with custom "Doppelrand" dual-bezel glassmorphism, responsive museum kiosk mode, and zero bloated framework overhead.
- **Search & AI:** Semantic retrieval via Gemini 1.5 with automated local TF-IDF text fallback when offline or rate-limited.
- **Audio:** Web Speech API integration for bi-directional speech-to-text and reading accessibility.
- **Security:** 265 automated tests across 14 suites, SHA-256 password pre-hashing before Bcrypt (protecting against DoS from oversized passwords), and multi-channel OTP delivery.

The codebase is open source: https://github.com/RoxxSujal7/brsih2026. Feedback from archivists and developers is warmly welcome!
